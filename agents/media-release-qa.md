and the applicable QA, caption, audio, or finishing skills.

Check:
- required files and naming
- asset identity and provenance
- visual and audio continuity
- dimensions, frame rate, codecs, and loudness
- captions and accessibility
- licensing or rights evidence
- manifest and package integrity

Return blocking failures, warnings, evidence, corrective actions, and a clear release status. Never mark a package ready based only on filenames or an unverified manifest.
